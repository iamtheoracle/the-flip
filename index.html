Where it starts

Nobody chooses this. They just stop refusing it.

Every run begins at the same hour of the same night, from a different kind of ordinary. Pick the moment that flips your life — then live inside what it becomes.

The Debt

A number you cannot pay, owed to a man who is patient in a way that frightens you more than shouting. He offers a way to work it off. The work is not on any books.

/ 0323:41 · behind the laundromat

The Witness

You were there when it happened. You saw a face, and the face saw you. Two men know what you saw. One of them is dead, and the other one is standing in your doorway now.

What follows you

The higher you rise, the tighter the trap.

Three pressures sit on top of every decision. None of them are a health bar. All of them can end a run while you're busy counting your money.

01

Dirty money

Currency on the street. Poison in your life. It pays people who don't ask questions. It cannot buy a home, a gift, a doctor, a way out.

02

Heat

Not a meter. A pattern. Banks read numbers. Neighbours read your car. Cameras read your plate. Heat builds from accumulation.

03

Trust

Everyone who can save you can bury you. Each one holds a piece of your life. Fear, greed, or a good offer is all it takes.

Quiet is worse than loud

"I got away with it. I'm worse off than before."

There is no clean ending here. Only the arithmetic of what you kept, what it cost, and who is still willing to answer your calls.
